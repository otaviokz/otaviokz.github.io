Privacy Policy

Safekeepr · Last updated: 21 August 2026

Safekeepr is a personal vault for storing sensitive information such as logins, credit cards, bank accounts, Wi‑Fi passwords, API keys and tokens, secure notes, and images. This policy explains what data the app handles and how it is protected. Safekeepr is designed around a simple principle: your secrets stay yours.

The short version
  • We do not operate any servers and we never receive, see, or collect your data.
  • Your entries are encrypted on your device before they are ever stored.
  • Nothing is shared with the developer, advertisers, or third parties.

Information You Store

Everything you save in Safekeepr — the contents of your logins, cards, accounts, notes, images, and other items — is entered by you and stored only in encrypted form. The developer has no access to this information and no ability to decrypt it.

How Your Data Is Protected

  • The sensitive contents of each item are encrypted on your device using a symmetric encryption key.
  • That encryption key is stored in the device Keychain, protected by your device passcode and, when you enable it, Face ID or Touch ID.
  • Face ID / Touch ID is used solely to unlock your encryption key locally on the device. Biometric data never leaves your device and is never available to the app or the developer.

iCloud Sync

If iCloud is enabled on your device, Safekeepr uses Apple CloudKit to sync your vault across your own Apple devices. This data is stored in your personal iCloud (private database) account and is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. The encrypted item contents are synced in their encrypted form. The developer has no access to your iCloud data. You can disable syncing at any time by turning off iCloud for Safekeepr in your device settings.

Camera

Safekeepr can use your device camera to scan a credit card and fill in the details automatically. This scanning happens entirely on your device. Camera images are used only to read the card details and are not transmitted anywhere or stored outside the encrypted item you choose to save.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising identifiers.
  • No personal information sent to the developer or any third‑party service.
  • No usage profiles, and no sale or sharing of data.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device (and in your iCloud account, if syncing is enabled) until you delete it. Deleting an item removes it from the app, and deleting the app removes its local data from the device. To remove synced copies, delete the data while signed in to iCloud or remove the app’s data from your iCloud storage.

Children’s Privacy

Safekeepr is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.
