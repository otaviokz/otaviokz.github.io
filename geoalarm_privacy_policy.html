Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

GeoAlarm is designed to work entirely on your device. We built it so your places and movements stay private.

What we collect

Nothing. GeoAlarm does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. We have no analytics, no ads, and no third‑party trackers.

Location

Your location is used only on your device to detect when you arrive near a place you saved. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else. Geofences are monitored by iOS on your behalf.

Your data

The alarms and places you create are stored locally on your device (and, if enabled, in your private iCloud/app container). You can delete them at any time by removing an alarm or deleting the app.
